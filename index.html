How Crsek-09 works

Everyone is a player. In Duo and Squad you never control eleven cricketers. You control one: the one whose turn it is.

  • Batting: only the striker on strike bats. Drag the stick to aim, tap a shot — Drive and Loft go straight, Pull goes leg side, Cut goes behind point, Sweep goes behind square. Timing beats power: swing as the ball reaches you, and watch the bounce.
  • Non-striker: call the extra run with RUN while the ball is live.
  • Bowling: one bowler per over. Pick line with the stick, pick length and type, then stop the release meter in the green.
  • Fielding: you are given one fielder. Move with the stick, tap CATCH when the ball comes to you, DIVE to extend your reach. When you pick the ball up, throw at either end to go for a run-out.
  • Camera: the ◎ button cycles broadcast, batter and top-down. The 3D/2D button switches between the stadium and the classic flat view.
  • Keeper is always AI so nobody is stuck behind the stumps.
